### 1.1 CALENDLY

**Founded:** 2013 | **HQ:** Atlanta, GA
**Valuation:** $3B (2021) | **Revenue:** $276.1M (2023)
**Users:** 10M+ active users, 100K+ organizations

**Target Market & Positioning:**
- General-purpose scheduling tool for professionals, sales teams, recruiters, and enterprises

| Plan | Price | Key Features |
|------|-------|-------------|
| Free | $0 | 1 event type, basic scheduling |
| Standard | $10-12/mo/seat | Unlimited events, integrations (HubSpot, Stripe, PayPal), group events |

**Key Differentiators:**
- Extreme simplicity; "just works"

- 70% of new users came from existing users sharing their links
- $100K ARR by end of 2014, $1M ARR by end of 2015
- Scaled to $70M ARR on just $550K seed funding
- 5-6% monthly growth during COVID pandemic

**Current Marketing Strategies:**

- LinkedIn and Google Ads targeting decision-makers in scheduling-heavy industries
- Webinars and educational content for thought leadership
- Replacing "Contact Us" forms with Calendly links increased sales-qualified opportunities by 40%
- Content marketing following PLG principles: keep user on page, provide reason to join, frictionless signup
- Minimal paid advertising historically

**Social Media:**
- Instagram: ~22K followers
- LinkedIn: Active presence, posts about integrations to activate power users
- YouTube: Educational content, product tutorials

**Content Strategy:**
- Blog focused on productivity, scheduling best practices

## 3. GROWTH STRATEGIES & TACTICS {#growth}

### How the Top Platforms Acquired Their First Users

| Platform | Primary Acquisition Strategy |
|----------|------------------------------|
| Calendly | Viral loop (every shared link = free ad), 70% users from referrals |
| Fresha | Free software -> marketplace -> commission model |
| Booksy | Mobile-first + M&A (acquired Genbook, Kiute) + influencer partnerships |
| Treatwell | Aggressive M&A across Europe + performance marketing |
| StyleSeat | Targeted underserved independent stylists + marketplace |
| GlossGenius | Design-forward product + word-of-mouth in beauty communities |
| Boulevard | Premium positioning + medspa market expansion |
| Mindbody | Direct sales team + industry events (BOLD conference) + ClassPass acquisition |
| Square Appointments | Leveraged existing Square payment user base |
| Acuity | Acquired by Squarespace -> instant distribution |

### Two-Sided Marketplace Best Practices
1. **Start supply-side:** High-quality providers naturally attract consumers (not vice versa)
2. **Geographic focus:** Achieve liquidity in one tightly defined area first
3. **Network effects:** More providers -> more clients -> more providers
4. **Balance:** Excess demand = empty shelves; excess supply = churn
5. **Trust mechanisms:** Reviews, ratings, verification

### Common Growth Hacking Tactics Used
1. **Freemium as trojan horse:** Calendly, Fresha, Setmore, Square all use free tiers
2. **Viral loops:** Calendly's scheduling links, "Powered by X" badges
3. **Marketplace commissions on NEW clients only:** Fresha (20%), Treatwell (35%), StyleSeat (30% capped at $50)
4. **Referral programs:** Both B2B (pro refers pro) and B2C (client refers client)
5. **Influencer partnerships:** Booksy, Fresha (Huda Beauty investment)
6. **M&A for market expansion:** Treatwell, Booksy acquired competitors
7. **Content marketing:** Blog educating professionals on business growth
8. **Instagram "Book Now" integration:** Booksy, Fresha, Vagaro all offer this
9. **Dynamic/smart pricing:** StyleSeat, Treatwell (demand-based pricing)
10. **Community building:** Mindbody's BOLD conference, educational programs

### Key Insights for Aliax.io Strategy

1. **Freemium is the winning acquisition model** - Fresha, Calendly, and Square all grew fastest with free tiers. The free tier should be genuinely useful, not crippled.

2. **AgendaPro is your direct competitor** - They have $35M in funding, 20K+ businesses in LATAM, and are expanding in Mexico City. Aliax.io needs a clear differentiator.

3. **WhatsApp > SMS in LATAM** - All US platforms focus on SMS; LATAM needs WhatsApp-first communication. AgendaPro already has this. This is table stakes for Mexico.

4. **Marketplace is the moat** - Fresha, Booksy, and Treatwell all proved that a consumer marketplace with commission on new clients is the most powerful growth engine. The marketplace creates network effects that are hard to replicate.

5. **Start supply-side, geographically focused** - Launch in ONE city (e.g., Mexico City or Guadalajara), get density of providers, THEN market to consumers.

6. **Commission on new clients only** - Fresha's model (20% on first marketplace booking, 0% after) is the gold standard. It aligns incentives: platform only makes money when it delivers real value.

7. **Content marketing in Spanish** - There's a gap in Spanish-language beauty business education content. Blog, YouTube, Instagram in Spanish could capture organic traffic.

8. **Instagram integration is mandatory** - All major beauty platforms offer Instagram "Book Now" buttons. Beauty professionals live on Instagram.

9. **Micro-influencer partnerships** - In Mexico, partner with local beauty influencers (stylists with 5K-50K followers) rather than expensive macro-influencers.

10. **Design matters** - GlossGenius grew to $100M partly because their product is beautiful. Beauty professionals care deeply about aesthetics.

### Potential Differentiators for Aliax.io
- **LATAM-first design:** WhatsApp, local payment methods (OXXO, SPEI, Mercado Pago), Spanish-first
- **Lower price point:** Undercut AgendaPro while offering more value
- **Community-driven:** Build the "Mindbody BOLD" equivalent for LATAM beauty professionals
- **AI in Spanish:** AI-powered client communication, scheduling, and marketing in Spanish
- **Professional profiles as micro-websites:** Reduce dependency on separate website/social media
